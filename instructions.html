How to Play

State the Location

1

Game Objective

Your goal is to identify all 50 U.S. states by clicking on their correct locations on the map. A state name or capital will appear in the yellow box - find it on the map and click the gray dot! This is recommended to be played on a computer or larger screen for the best experience, but it can also be played on mobile devices (landscape mode).

2

How to Play

Click the correct state: When you see a state name or capital in the yellow box, click on the corresponding gray dot on the map. If correct, the dot will show the state's postal code (like "CA" for California).

Wrong answer? Click the wrong state and you'll get a 3-second time penalty, and the button will flash red.

3

Timer & Controls

The timer starts automatically when you begin. You can pause the game anytime using the Pause button, which will disable all state buttons. Click Start to resume. Try to complete all 50 states as quickly as possible!

4

Track Your Progress

Watch the progress bar at the top left fill up as you identify states. The counter shows how many states you've completed (e.g., "25/50"). Once you reach 50/50, you win and the timer stops automatically!
